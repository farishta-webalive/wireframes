How RenoLogic works for you

Your end-to-end journey on the platform.

1
Verify your business
Upload your licence, insurance and ABN docs from your dashboard. RenoLogic Admin reviews them.
2
Activate your subscription
Once approved, subscribe from inside the platform — no payment required before approval.
3
Browse open jobs
Filter by trade and location, signal interest. No pricing or messaging at this stage.
4
Submit a quote when selected
If the homeowner picks your firm, the Quote Builder unlocks. Define line items and milestones.
5
Deliver, evidence, record
Upload evidence, mark milestones complete, record payments, manage to handover.
You do not submit pricing or quotes before the homeowner selects your firm. No pricing is visible to any party before selection.
